Build a modern, viral-looking website for a service called **NPC Google Reviews**.

## BUSINESS CONCEPT

NPC Google Reviews creates short-form, entertaining NPC-style videos for local businesses that can be used on Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, websites, and marketing campaigns.

The concept:
A creator performs an exaggerated NPC-style reaction/review of a business, product, food, service, or experience. The goal is to create memorable social content while helping businesses generate attention and authentic-looking promotional content.

The website should feel like a **viral Instagram-first creative agency**, NOT like a traditional corporate marketing agency.
